## Search & Export Example

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Google Sheet">
    - Upload your service account JSON
    - Paste Google Sheet URL
    - Click "Test Spreadsheet Connection"
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your search">
    - Enter a company name or ticker (e.g., `AAPL`)
    - Pick filing types (e.g., 10-K)
    - Set the date range
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch and export">
    - Click "Fetch Filings"
    - Review results in the table
    - Download as CSV or auto-export to sheet
  </Step>
</Steps>
