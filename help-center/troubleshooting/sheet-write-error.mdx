---
title: Failed to Write to Google Sheet
description: Quickly resolve common worksheet writing issues.
---

## Symptoms

- Error: "Failed to write to spreadsheet"
- No data appears in Google Sheet after fetch

## Steps to Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check sharing">
    Ensure your service account email is shared on the Google Sheet (check `client_email` in JSON).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate credentials">
    Confirm your Google service account JSON is uploaded and valid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Sheet URL">
    Double-check the spreadsheet URL and worksheet title are correct in the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>
